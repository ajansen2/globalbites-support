Delete your Global Bites account

In the app

Open Settings → Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps. Settings is accessible from your Kitchen/profile area in the reviewed app. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your account.

If you cannot use the app

Email adam@argora.ai with “Global Bites account deletion” in the subject. Tell us which account you mean. We may ask you to verify ownership, but do not send a password or identity document in your first message. We will explain any further steps and the expected completion time.

What the process removes

Account deletion removes the account and account-owned records from the active app database, including the profile, recipes, posts, comments, sent messages, saved items, pantry records, check-ins, follows and notification tokens. References in some shared records are removed rather than deleting another person’s record.

Separate cleanup and records

Uploaded media is queued for separate cleanup; deletion does not instantly erase every stored copy. Media still referenced by surviving records, and media whose ownership cannot be established, may need further handling. Limited deletion and cleanup records can retain the former account identifier and processing status.

Provider backups, logs, analytics and crash records have their own retention and deletion processes. Copies other people have saved or exported are outside our control. Contact support if you need help with a specific item or provider record.

Newsletter signups and restaurant claims stored separately by email are not automatically deleted with an app account. Ask support to include these records in a verified deletion request.
