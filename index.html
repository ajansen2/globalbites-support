Global Bites Support

Cook, share and discover food together. Global Bites offers recipe sharing, restaurant discovery, city food chat, Dish of the Month and Saffron cooking assistance. Feature availability depends on your app version.

Get help or report a problem

Email adam@argora.ai with a short description and your app version. You can include a screenshot if useful; remove private messages, precise locations and other unnecessary personal details first. Never send your password.

For inappropriate content or unwanted contact, use the report or block controls offered in the app, or contact support. You can also contact us to appeal a moderation decision.

Your account and privacy

Open Settings → Delete Account to request account deletion in the app. Uninstalling alone does not delete your account. See Account deletion for what happens next, and our Privacy Policy for data practices and requests.

AI features

Saffron and recipe scanning/import features use external AI services. The Privacy Policy explains which providers receive the conversation, photos or saved context used for each feature. AI suggestions can be wrong; check ingredients and food-safety instructions before relying on them.
