Global Bites

Cook, share & discover food — together.

Global Bites is a community for food lovers: share your recipes, build cookbooks, discover restaurants around the world, chat about food with your city, and compete in the monthly Dish of the Month.

What you can do

  • Post recipes — type them, snap a photo, or import from a link
  • Discover and check in at restaurants near you
  • Join your city's food chat
  • Enter Dish of the Month cooking competitions
  • Ask Saffron, your AI cooking assistant

Support
