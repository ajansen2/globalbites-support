Global Bites Privacy Policy

Effective date: [to be set when published].

Global Bites is operated under the name Cidron Interactive in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. This policy explains how we handle information in the Global Bites app and on our support website. Contact Adam Jansen at adam@argora.ai with privacy questions, including questions about providers outside Canada.

Information we collect and why

  • Account and profile: email address, username, account identifier, sign-in information, and profile details you provide, such as a photo, name, bio, cuisine preferences and dietary restrictions. Passwords are stored as hashes. Social sign-in supplies identifiers, tokens and profile information authorized by the provider. We use this information for sign-in, your profile and app features.
  • Your activity and content: recipes, images and videos, saved recipes and collections, comments, ratings, posts, city chat and direct messages, follows, restaurant saves and check-ins, pantry items, cooking entries and preferences. We use these to provide the features you choose, personalize the app and moderate reported content. Dietary details can reveal health information or beliefs; provide only what you want used for these features.
  • Location: when you allow device location access, we send coordinates to our server to find nearby restaurants and, when needed, to Google Places. Nearby-search coordinates may remain in area caches and technical logs; location processing is not limited to the instant of your search. Restaurant check-ins and any city or location you post also form part of your saved or shared activity.
  • Photos and camera: images or videos you choose to upload for recipes, posts, profiles or other features. AI photo features also send the selected image to the AI provider described below. An image can reveal people, places or personal details.
  • Usage and diagnostics: app interactions, screen views, content identifiers, device/app information, installation or device identifiers used by our providers, crash reports and push-notification tokens. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics can receive your Global Bites account identifier, so these records are not described as anonymous. We use them to understand usage and investigate failures.
  • Cookbooks you own, want and sell: the printed cookbooks you add (title, edition, condition, your notes and the photos you take), your shelf if you choose to show it on your profile, listings you submit (price, condition and the general pickup area you enter), wanted-book requests (a book or an author, your budget and pickup area or province) and descriptions of what is inside a book that you submit. When you ask about a copy on someone's shelf ("I'd like this copy"), we record your request for our team. We use this information to show your collection, review listings and look for matches between members.
  • Sales arranged through Global Bites: when our team arranges a sale, we record the book, the price, our fee (no fee is charged during the Edmonton launch), the province, the dates, which members sold and bought it, and whether the buyer confirmed it arrived. Each copy keeps a short ownership history, shown as "private collector, Alberta, 2026", or with your username only if you choose to be named. To arrange a sale you started, our team may email you from adam@argora.ai.
  • Book catalogue lookups: when you look up a book by ISBN, we send the ISBN to Open Library and keep the answer, with a record of which member asked.
  • Support and service records: your correspondence, report details, ordinary connection information and records needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service. Our support mailbox and website host also process information needed to receive messages and serve pages.

Who can see your content

Your public profile and content you post publicly can be seen by other users. In Cookbooks, other members can see approved listings and the shelves members choose to show; a shelf never shows a price or a pickup area. Sellers do not see who wants their book, members do not message each other about books, and we never pass one member's contact details to another: our team arranges each sale. City chat is shared with the relevant community; direct messages are delivered to participants. Use the privacy controls offered for a feature and check the audience before sharing. People who receive content may save or copy it. Authorized personnel may access relevant records to answer support requests, moderate reports, maintain the service or investigate abuse.

AI cooking and recipe features

Saffron text chat: we send the conversation submitted for the request, together with ingredient names saved in your pantry and your saved dietary preferences or restrictions, to DeepSeek. The service can fall back to Anthropic (Claude) and then OpenAI if an earlier provider fails. A fallback can mean more than one provider processes a request. Saving a recipe extracted from Saffron chat also uses these text providers to process the selected chat content.

Saffron photo questions: the selected image, your prompt, and pantry and dietary context are sent to OpenAI for image analysis. Recipe photo scanning: the selected image or image URL is sent to OpenAI to extract recipe information. Recipe imports from links: fetched recipe text is processed by DeepSeek, with Anthropic as a fallback.

These providers process the submitted information to generate a response. The content itself can identify you even if your email address is not included in the request. We do not describe these requests as anonymous or promise that every provider immediately deletes them. Provider retention and other processing depend on the applicable API terms and settings. Avoid including unnecessary personal details. You can choose not to use AI features.

Sponsored placements

Some restaurant results and Discover cards are marked "Sponsored": a business paid for the placement. We choose which one to show only from the city you are browsing. We don't use your profile, your activity, your location history or any advertising identifier to choose them, and we don't share your information with advertisers. We count how many times each placement is shown and tapped per day, without recording who saw or tapped it. If you use "Report this ad", the report is kept with your account like other reports.

Service providers and other disclosures

  • Railway: application hosting and database processing.
  • Amazon Web Services S3: uploaded image and video storage.
  • Google Maps and Places: maps, restaurant information and location-based searches.
  • Google Firebase: Analytics for usage measurement, Crashlytics for diagnostics, and Cloud Messaging for push delivery; notifications also use the device platform’s delivery service.
  • DeepSeek, Anthropic and OpenAI: the AI processing described above.
  • Open Library (Internet Archive): book catalogue details for the ISBNs looked up; no member information is sent.
  • Apple, Google and Meta: sign-in when you choose the corresponding method.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting this support website. Our email provider processes support correspondence.

Providers handle relevant information under their applicable service terms and privacy practices. We may disclose information where required by law or when necessary to protect the service and people using it. We do not sell personal information.

Processing outside Canada

Our providers may process information outside Canada, where local laws and lawful-access powers can differ. Contact Adam Jansen at adam@argora.ai for information about our use of these providers.

Your choices and requests

You can edit your profile and use the content controls available in the app. Device settings let you withdraw location, camera, photo and notification permissions. Withdrawing a permission stops future access allowed by that permission; it does not delete previously uploaded information. Deleting the app does not delete your account.

To request access, correction, deletion, help withdrawing consent or an explanation of our practices, email adam@argora.ai. We may need to verify your identity or authority. Do not send passwords or identity documents in your first message. We will explain any applicable limits and respond within the period required by applicable law. You may raise a concern with the privacy regulator where you live.

Account deletion and retention

Open Settings → Delete Account in the app, or contact support for help. The account-deletion process removes your profile and account-owned content from the active database, including your recipes, posts, comments, sent messages, pantry records and social connections, and your cookbooks, shelf, listings and wanted-book requests. Some shared records have your account reference removed instead of deleting someone else’s record: a sale you took part in stays on record for the other member and for our business records, and a book's ownership history shows you as "former member".

Uploaded media is processed separately through a deletion queue. It is not erased from every storage system immediately. Media still referenced by a surviving record can remain pending further review or cleanup. We retain limited deletion and cleanup records, which can include your former account identifier and status. Separate email-only records, such as a newsletter signup or restaurant claim, require a verified request to support and are not automatically removed by deleting an app account.

Information is retained while needed for the feature or purpose described in this policy. Provider backups, operational logs, analytics and crash records have separate retention and deletion processes. Copies other people have saved or exported are outside our control. Any legally required retention must be limited to its applicable purpose. See Account deletion for help.

Children, security and changes

Global Bites is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has supplied personal information, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

We use safeguards including encrypted connections and access controls to protect information. No online service can guarantee complete security. Protect your account and device, and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

We will update this policy when our practices change and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required before introducing a new use. Acceptance of general terms or continued app use does not replace a specific consent where one is required.
