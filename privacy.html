Global Bites Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 8, 2026 Last updated: July 8, 2026

Global Bites is operated by Cidron Interactive ("we," "us") in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the Global Bites mobile app and how we use it.

Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account we collect your email address, username, display name, and password (stored as a secure hash). If you sign in with Apple, Google, or Facebook, we receive your name and email address from that provider.

Content you create. Recipes, photos, videos, comments, ratings, cookbook collections, chat and direct messages, restaurant check-ins, pantry items, and profile details (such as your avatar, bio, and signature dish). Content you post publicly — such as recipes, comments, and city chat messages — is visible to other users.

Location. With your permission, we use your device's location to show restaurants near you. You can disable location access at any time in your device settings; the app's other features continue to work.

Photos and camera. With your permission, we access your camera and photo library so you can add images to recipes, posts, and your profile. Photos are only uploaded when you choose to attach them.

Device and usage information. Device type, operating system version, app version, push notification tokens, crash reports, and analytics about how the app is used (such as which features are opened). We use this to fix bugs and improve the app.

How We Use Information

We use your information to operate the app: displaying your content to you and other users, showing nearby restaurants, sending push notifications you've enabled, providing the Saffron AI cooking assistant, running Dish of the Month competitions, keeping the community safe (including reviewing reported content), and improving features. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Saffron AI Assistant

Messages you send to Saffron, along with related information you choose to provide (such as pantry contents), are processed by a third-party artificial intelligence provider in order to generate responses. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in AI chat messages.

Service Providers

We share information with service providers only as needed to run the app:

  • Cloud hosting and database (Railway) — app data storage and processing
  • Amazon Web Services (S3) — storage of uploaded photos and videos
  • Google Maps and Google Places — restaurant information, maps, and

    • location features (subject to Google's Privacy Policy)

  • Firebase (Google) — push notifications and crash reporting
  • AI processing provider — Saffron assistant responses, as described above

These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Your Choices and Rights

Access and update. You can edit your profile and content in the app.

Delete your content. You can delete your own recipes, comments, and messages at any time.

Delete your account. You can permanently delete your account and associated personal information in the app under Settings → Delete Account, or by contacting us at the email below. Some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate purposes such as fraud prevention, and content you shared that others have interacted with (such as comments in a conversation) may be anonymized rather than removed.

Push notifications and location can be turned off in your device settings at any time.

Residents of certain regions may have additional rights (such as access, correction, portability, or objection) under laws including Canada's PIPEDA, Alberta's PIPA, the GDPR, or the CCPA. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

Data Retention

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

Children

Global Bites is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secured cloud infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We will post the updated version here and update the date above. Material changes will be communicated in the app.
