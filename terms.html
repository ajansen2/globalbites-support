Sales are between members. Global Bites helps members who own printed cookbooks find members who want them. Our team arranges each sale, but the sale is between the seller and the buyer: we don't own, hold or inspect the books, and the seller is responsible for describing a copy accurately. Global Bites provides no messaging between the buyer and the seller of a book and never passes one member's contact details to another. A sale that starts through Global Bites is completed through Global Bites.

Pickup. Books are handed over in person at local pickup. Meet in a public place, in daylight where you can, and look the book over before you pay. The buyer pays the seller the agreed price at pickup; Global Bites does not take or hold that payment.

Fees. When a fee applies, it comes out of the seller's price, never adds to what the buyer pays, and never takes more than a quarter of the price; listings start at $20. There is no fee during the Edmonton launch. Before any fee applies we will update these terms and tell you.

When something goes wrong. After pickup, the buyer confirms in the app that the book arrived as described; that is when the copy moves to the buyer's collection. If it isn't as described, or the pickup didn't happen, say so in the app instead and our team will help the two members sort it out. Because the price is paid in person, Global Bites can't refund it; any refund of the price is between the buyer and the seller. There is no Global Bites fee to refund during the launch.
